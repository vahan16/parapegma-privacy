The short version: Parapegma is free. It plans and prices trips, but it never sells you a ticket and never takes a payment. When you book, you book with the partner you choose, on their terms. Our prices are estimates — check them with the partner before you travel.

1. What the App does

Parapegma plans multi-city trips. It prices the flights, trains, buses and ferries for a whole journey, ranks whole trips against each other, and keeps the trips you save on your phone. It searches direct flights from your home city and routes the rest overland. Its totals cover getting from place to place only — not beds, meals or entry tickets.

2. Prices and timetables are estimates

  • Fares, times and routes come from third-party sources and change constantly. Until a partner confirms a price, the App’s figure is an estimate and may be wrong, out of date or unavailable.
  • You can edit times and fares yourself; the App then works with what you entered.
  • Totals are shown in the currency set in Settings when the trip was priced and are not converted afterwards.
  • The App is a planning aid, not professional travel advice. Before you travel, check schedules, prices, visa and entry rules, health requirements and travel advisories with the carrier and the official sources.

3. Bookings are with partners, not with us

When you choose to book, the App opens a partner’s site or app (for example Aviasales, Klook, Tiqets, Hotellook, Booking.com, Ferryhopper, Kiwitaxi or Omio). Any booking you make there is a contract between you and that partner or the carrier, under their terms and privacy policy. They set the final price and availability, take your payment, and handle changes, cancellations, refunds and complaints. We are not a party to that booking and cannot change, cancel or refund it.

Once you mark a leg as booked through a partner, the App stops letting you edit that leg, so your plan matches what you actually bought.

4. Affiliate links

Links to partners go through Travelpayouts. If you book through one, we may earn a commission from the partner. It costs you nothing extra, and it never affects how the App ranks trips or which options it shows you.

5. No payments, no accounts, no ads

The App is free. It has no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising, and it never asks for or takes a payment. You don’t need an account to use it.

6. Your trips and cloud backup

Your trips are kept on your phone. If you switch on cloud backup in Settings, a copy is kept on our server under a random recovery code. Anyone who has that code can read the trips filed under it, so keep it to yourself. If you lose your phone and your code with backup off, or delete the app with backup off, we cannot recover your trips. How the App handles your data is described in the Privacy Policy, which is part of these terms.
